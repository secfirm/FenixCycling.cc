Service Agreement

By bringing your bicycle to FENIX Cycling for service, you agree to the terms and conditions outlined in this document. These terms apply to all repairs, maintenance, and service work performed at our shop located at 23681 E La Palma Ave, Suite B, Yorba Linda, CA 92887.

Deposit Policy

50% Deposit Required For:

  • Repairs requiring special order parts (custom components not in stock)
  • Major repairs (frame damage, suspension overhauls, complete drivetrain replacements)
  • E-bike electrical work or motor service

Deposit amount: 50% of the estimated repair cost

Balance due: Remaining 50% is due at pickup

Purpose: The deposit ensures we have the funds to order parts immediately and complete your work efficiently. We do not finance repair work.

Repair Estimates & Approval

We will provide a detailed written estimate for any repair before work begins, including:

  • Description of the work to be performed
  • Estimated cost and timeline
  • Cost of parts (if applicable)
  • Labor charges

Work will not proceed without your approval. If estimates change due to additional issues discovered during inspection, we will contact you to confirm new pricing before proceeding.

Pickup & Pickup Timeline

30-Day Pickup Window

Your bike must be picked up within 30 days of our notification that it is ready for pickup.

How we notify you:

  • Phone call to the number provided at check-in
  • Email to the email address provided
  • Text message (SMS) if you have opted in to receive messages

Storage fees: After 30 days, a storage fee may apply ($10/week or portion thereof) for continued storage of your bike at our facility.

Abandoned bikes: Bikes left beyond 60 days may be considered abandoned and handled accordingly.

Communication & Notifications

FENIX Cycling will keep you informed at every step of your repair:

  • Inspection complete – We'll contact you with findings and recommendations
  • Ready to proceed? – We'll confirm approval before starting work
  • Parts ordered – If special parts are needed, we'll let you know ETA
  • Ready for pickup – We'll notify you when your bike is complete and ready to collect

You may opt to receive these notifications by phone, email, and/or SMS text message. See our Privacy Policy for SMS opt-in/opt-out details.

Payment & Accepted Methods

We accept:

  • Cash
  • Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
  • Debit cards
  • Mobile payments (if available)

Payment is due in full upon pickup, unless other arrangements have been agreed to in advance.

Liability & Damage Disclaimer

FENIX Cycling is not responsible for:

  • Personal items left on bikes – Lights, computers, water bottles, bags, phone mounts, and other accessories should be removed before leaving your bike. The shop is not responsible for theft or damage to personal items left on bicycles.
  • Pre-existing damage – Hidden or pre-existing damage discovered during service will be discussed with you before repair work proceeds
  • Normal wear and tear – Some components may wear or fail beyond our control
  • Bikes left beyond 60 days – Abandoned bikes may be disposed of or repurposed

Warranty on Repairs

We stand behind our work. All parts installed during repair come with manufacturer warranties (typically 1–5 years depending on the part). Our labor warranty covers defects in workmanship for 30 days after pickup. If an issue arises from our work within 30 days, bring your bike back and we will address it.

Warranty does not cover:

  • Damage from crashes, accidents, or misuse
  • Failure to perform routine maintenance (cleaning, lubrication)
  • Natural wear and tear on consumable parts (brake pads, chain, cables)

Service Area

FENIX Cycling primarily serves Yorba Linda, Corona, Anaheim Hills, and surrounding communities in Orange County, California. We welcome riders from all areas, and special service (like home pickup & delivery for major repairs) is available upon request and approval.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Before work begins: If you decide to cancel before work starts, your deposit (if paid) will be refunded in full minus any costs incurred (parts ordered, administrative fees).

After work begins: Once repair work has started, cancellation is not available. Your deposit is non-refundable.

Defective work: If we determine our work is faulty, we will redo the work at no charge within 30 days of pickup.

Limitation of Liability

FENIX Cycling's liability for any repair work is limited to the total cost of the repair. We do not accept liability for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages (such as loss of use or inconvenience).
